Privacy Policy

Brainsplash Uploader · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Brainsplash Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Brainsplash (@BrainsplashQuiz). It is used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your information is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the signed-in user's permission, the app:

  • uploads videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags and publishing schedule, to the signed-in YouTube channel; and
  • reads basic information about that channel (such as its name) to confirm that the correct channel is connected.

The app does not access, collect or process data about any other YouTube users.

What is stored and shared

The only data the app stores is the Google sign-in token, kept on the channel owner's own computer so that scheduled uploads can run. No YouTube data is stored on servers, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

How to revoke access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings → Third-party access. Deleting the stored token from the computer also stops the app from accessing the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
