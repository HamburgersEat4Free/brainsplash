What is Brainsplash?

Brainsplash is a YouTube channel that posts a new quiz Short every day: guess the flag, geography challenges and general knowledge, from easy to impossible, with 3 seconds per question.

Brainsplash Uploader

Brainsplash Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Brainsplash channel. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.
